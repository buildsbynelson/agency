Frequently Asked Questions

We’re here to answer your questions and provide clarity, ensuring you have all the information you need.

1. How much does it cost to start a project?

Straight answer: our projects start from ₦750,000. Where it goes from there depends on what you need. A landing page is very different from a full website with brand identity and custom features. We don't do vague pricing. Once you tell us what you're building, we'll send you a clear breakdown with no surprises.

2. How long does it take to complete a project?

Most websites and brand projects are delivered within 2 to 4 weeks. Larger or more complex builds can take 6 to 8 weeks. The biggest factor that affects timelines is how quickly you can provide feedback and content. The faster you move, the faster we deliver.

3. Will my website look good on mobile?

Every site we build is fully responsive across phones, tablets, and desktops. We design mobile-first, meaning your site is optimised for the device most of your customers are already using before we even think about desktop.

4. What happens after my website is launched?

We don't just hand over the files and disappear. Every project includes a full handover walkthrough so you know exactly how to manage your site. We also offer ongoing support and maintenance for clients who want us to stay in their corner long after launch.

5. Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build is set up with the fundamentals of SEO including proper page structure, fast load speeds, meta descriptions, and mobile optimisation. This gives you a solid foundation to rank. For clients who want to go deeper with SEO strategy, that is a conversation we can have.

6. Do you require full payment upfront?

No. We keep it simple and fair. We require a 50% deposit to get your project started and the remaining 50% is due on completion before final files and access are handed over. This way you are protected, we are protected, and everyone is committed to getting the best outcome.
